**`dossier`** — a dossier has two genres, and the skill only knew one. Adds the research/decision genre split.

- **Preflight gains a fourth check: genre.** Does the reader want to learn the state of a topic and conclude for themselves, or to be handed a ranked recommendation? Infer when the request makes it obvious; **ask when it doesn't**. The existing three checks test whether a request is *actionable* — "evaluate this pitch" passes all three cleanly while leaving the genre wide open, which is exactly how a survey request becomes an adjudication. Names the four ambiguity smells, and defaults to **research** when asking isn't possible, because that is the recoverable error: a missing recommendation can be appended in minutes, while an unwanted adjudication has to be rebuilt.
- **New `templates/report.md` for the research genre.** Survey spine, subject-shaped headings, an evidence-grading section, a required *"what could not be determined"* section, and an **optional assessment confined to the end** — so a reader can take the research and skip the opinion.
- **`EVALUATE` gains a survey mode.** It previously offered scoring as its only analysis mode, which answers "which is best" — the wrong question for a survey. Survey mode instead maps the field with a stated inclusion rule, builds a taxonomy that carries explanatory weight, grades evidence per claim, and records gaps explicitly.
- **Genre now has consequences instead of being a label.** `SCOPE` previously offered "comparison, evaluation, or investigation" but nothing downstream read it. Genre now selects the template and switches EVALUATE and SYNTHESIZE. The Key Facts box follows: scope/method/evidence-quality/research-window for research, who-decides/decision-model/deadline for decisions — and an empty decision model is called out as a signal the genre was picked wrong.
- **Guidance on headings and the opening.** Research headings name subjects, not stances — a reader skims by heading to find a topic, and stances give them nothing to skim for. The first section is a lede (what is true), not a verdict.
- **Review-checklist item 11, genre match**, to be run **first**: it is the only item whose failure means rebuilding rather than editing. Four new Common Mistakes rows, including compiled reports that fail to link the research files they were built from.

**Not a revival of `dossier-framing-declared.sh`** (removed 2026-04-18). Different axis: those modes classified the character of the *sources*, genre classifies what document the *reader* wants — so genre was never covered by it. Different mechanism: that gate linted a declared label that changed no output, which is why it was ignorable. Deliberately no script here; "does this document's shape match what was asked for" is a judgement, not a pattern match.

# Source Archive

Local captures of sources cited by the dossier in this folder, for
offline verification after link rot. Depth-0 captures — the cited page
only, never a site mirror.

`Accessed` is the UTC date the capture was taken. `SHA-256` is over the
captured file, so a later re-fetch can be compared against what was cited.

Status values: `ok`, `thin-capture` (page is likely JS-rendered — the
capture is a shell; re-capture manually with a browser or single-file-cli),
`capped` (exceeded the size ceiling; not stored), `unavailable` (fetch failed).
